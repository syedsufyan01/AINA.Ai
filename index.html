Common Civic Myths vs. Pakistani Legal Reality

❌ MYTH

"Police have the legal discretion to refuse an FIR and only write a Roznamcha report."

✅ LEGAL REALITY

Under Section 154 of the Code of Criminal Procedure (Cr.P.C), the Station House Officer (SHO) is statutorily commanded to register an FIR whenever information disclosing a cognizable offense (theft, robbery, snatching, assault) is provided. Refusal constitutes dereliction of official duty, and a citizen can immediately petition the Sessions Judge as Ex-Officio Justice of Peace under Section 22-A & 22-B Cr.P.C.

❌ MYTH

"Electricity DISCOs can slap detection bills without physical meter verification."

✅ LEGAL REALITY

Under NEPRA Consumer Service Manual Chapter 6, no consumer can be billed detection units without a joint physical inspection in the consumer's presence, an independent laboratory report verifying meter tampering, and issuance of a prior 7-day show cause notice. Unilateral detection bills are consistently quashed by the Federal Ombudsman (Wafaqi Mohtasib).

❌ MYTH

"Filing a complaint before the Federal Ombudsman requires hiring a lawyer and paying fees."

✅ LEGAL REALITY

Complaints before the Wafaqi Mohtasib (Federal Ombudsman) are 100% free of charge. No court fees, stamp papers, or legal representation are required. Any citizen can file online (mohtasib.gov.pk) or by plain paper against any federal department, and decisions are rendered within 60 days.

What is AINA?

AINA (AI National Assistant) is an AI-powered civic platform designed to help Pakistani citizens navigate everyday public, administrative, and legal challenges. By understanding inquiries in simple Urdu, English, or Roman Urdu, AINA categorizes issues, identifies competent authorities, provides realistic resolution roadmaps, and formats official complaint drafts.

💡 Our Focus in the Hackathon

We built a robust Minimum Viable Product (MVP) featuring core capabilities:

  • Intelligent Issue Understanding & Categorization: Covers Utilities, NADRA, Police, Cybercrime, WASA, Municipal, and Consumer Protection.
  • Bilingual Multimodal Accessibility: Voice input (Urdu & English), speech narration, and full Nastaliq typography support.
  • Actionable 4-Phase Resolution Roadmap: Step-by-step guidance including evidence preservation, first-line submission, and quasi-judicial escalation.
  • Official Smart Complaint Drafter: Ready-to-file legal petitions in English and Urdu with live editing and PDF/Print export.
  • Civic Directory: Verified emergency helplines and direct links to official government complaint cells.
📌 Important Hackathon Disclaimer: AINA is an independent civic tech innovation platform, not an official government entity. It equips citizens with knowledge and structured drafts, while the user remains responsible for filing and submission.
